Nightingale

Six calibrated risk models, running in your browser, each printed with the interval it earned.

Read this first

These are screening-triage risk models trained on small public research datasets. They are not diagnostic devices and must not be used for medical decisions. The diabetes labels are self-reported survey responses. The cervical-cancer cohort has 55 positive biopsies.

Ask a model a question

The trees, the calibrator and the conformal threshold are all in this page. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere to send it. Leave any field blank and the model routes that feature down its missing branch — a blank is not measured, never zero.

    Plate II Calibration & decision

    The calibration lab

    Discrimination asks whether the model ranks patients correctly. Calibration asks whether a stated 20% means twenty in a hundred. A model can be excellent at the first and useless at the second, so both are on the plate, alongside the only question a clinic actually asks: at what threshold is acting on this model better than acting on nobody, or on everybody?

    Provenance Check it yourself

    Verify every artifact

    Each artifact is SHA-256 hashed into a manifest and the manifest is signed with an Ed25519 key that never enters the repository. This recomputes every hash and checks the signature.

    pip install -e . && nightingale verify

    Confirm the public key

    openssl pkey -pubin -in provenance/pubkey.pem \
  -outform DER | tail -c 32 | shasum -a 256
