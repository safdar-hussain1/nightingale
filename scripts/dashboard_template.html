A research demo, not a medical device. These are screening-triage risk models trained on small public research datasets. They are not diagnostic devices and must not be used for medical decisions. The diabetes labels are self-reported survey responses. The cervical-cancer cohort has 55 positive biopsies.

How it works

Four steps run every time you change an answer, all of them inside this page. The pictures are drawn from the patient in the demo above.

  1. 1

    Your answers become a row of numbers

    Each answer is written into the exact columns the model was trained on. A blank stays blank: it means not measured, never zero, and every tree has its own route for it.

  2. 2

    A forest of small trees votes

    The model is gradient-boosted decision trees, exported from Python to plain JSON and walked by a short script in this page. Each tree adds a small push up or down; together they make the raw score.

  3. 3

    Calibration turns the vote into a risk

    A raw score is not a risk you can read as a percentage. Calibration maps it to how often patients with that score really had the condition, in records the model never trained on.

  4. 4

    A conformal check gives an honest verdict

    Likely, unlikely, or too close to call. The model keeps every answer it cannot rule out at its confidence level, so when both answers stay possible it says so instead of guessing.

All six models at a glance

    Calibration, and when acting on the model pays

    Ranking asks whether the model puts patients in the right order. Calibration asks whether a stated 20% means twenty in a hundred. A model can be good at the first and poor at the second, so both are here, beside the question a clinic actually asks: at what threshold does acting on this model beat acting on everyone, or on no one?

    Provenance

    Verify every artifact

    Each artifact is SHA-256 hashed into a manifest, and the manifest is signed with an Ed25519 key that never enters the repository. This recomputes every hash and checks the signature.

    pip install -e . && nightingale verify

    Confirm the public key

    openssl pkey -pubin -in provenance/pubkey.pem \
  -outform DER | tail -c 32 | shasum -a 256
